# Support playbook

When a customer asks for a person, the assistant records an escalation and stops proposing account changes. Internal notes stay on the ticket and are not pasted back into the customer transcript.

The playbook covers weekday support hours from 09:00 to 17:00 in the tenant's local time. Outside those hours the assistant says a person will reply on the next staffed day.

Escalation does not approve a refund, a return, or an address change. Those actions stay with the application rules.
