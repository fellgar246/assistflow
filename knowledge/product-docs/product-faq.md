# Product FAQ

Harbor Goods products include a one-year limited warranty against defects in materials. Spare parts for the listed catalog items can be requested through support after the order number is confirmed.

Care instructions: wipe the item with a dry cloth and do not submerge it. The warranty does not cover damage from liquid immersion or from a missing order number.

This FAQ does not set return windows, refund amounts, or shipping cutoffs. Those rules live in the published policies.
