# Shipping address changes

An address change is eligible while the shipment is pending, in transit, or out for delivery, and the estimated delivery date is still today or later.

Delivered shipments and shipments in exception are not eligible. Cancelled orders are not eligible.

The cutoff date is the estimated delivery date. The demo shipment for order ORD-10482 leaves the DFW hub and remains eligible because its estimated delivery date is 2099-06-15.
