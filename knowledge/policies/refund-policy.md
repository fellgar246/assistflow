# Refund policy

A refund request can be recorded for a paid or fulfilled order. The requested amount cannot exceed the order total minus refund requests that are still requested, pending review, or approved. Rejected refund requests do not reduce the remaining amount.

The reply states the maximum amount in minor currency units. A refund request is not a payment capture and never includes a payment instrument.

Recording a refund request does not send money. A person reviews the request before any payment change.
